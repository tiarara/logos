# Post-call wrap-up

Use this after a client call, when Tiara asks for a wrap-up, recap, follow-up or next steps. The goal is that nothing said on the call gets lost, and that the next prep starts from an accurate picture.

## 1. Find the call

- Find the recording in Fathom with `list_meetings` (latest first, with summaries and action items). Match it by title, invitees or time against the calendar event. Recordings sometimes appear as "Impromptu Google Meet Meeting".
- If there's no recording yet, it may still be processing. Tell Tiara, and offer to work from her notes instead or try again later.
- Find the prep page for this call, if there is one, so you can compare what was planned with what happened.

## 2. Read the full transcript

Extract:

- **Decisions**: what was agreed or approved, and what changed (priorities, scope, design).
- **Action items**: owner, what, and due date if one was said. Keep client-owned and NxtLayr-owned items apart.
- **Feedback on delivered work**: what worked, what didn't, and requested changes, with quotes.
- **New requirements or opportunities**: anything that could be the next build. Frame these as proposals for Chris to approve, since he approves scope changes.
- **Value data**: any time or cost figures the client gave (how long something takes now, or how long it used to take), for the client value ledger.
- **Next meeting**: whether one was booked, and when.
- **Prep vs reality**: which planned questions were answered, and which open items are still open.

## 3. Output

Add a **Wrap-up** section to the prep page, or publish a new page if there wasn't one. It should have:

1. A 3–5 line summary.
2. Decisions.
3. Action items in two columns (client and NxtLayr), with owners and dates.
4. Updated "who owes what".
5. Feedback and requested changes.
6. New opportunities, marked "proposed, needs Chris's OK".
7. Value-ledger numbers, if any.
8. The next meeting, or a reminder to book one.
9. A short brief for Chris: 3–4 lines Tiara can paste into Slack.

## 4. Follow-up email (draft only)

Draft a short follow-up email to the client from tiara@nxtlayrai.com, in plain Australian English. Thank them, list what was agreed, what they're sending, what we're doing, and when we'll meet next. Save it as a **draft** in the work Gmail account (Composio `gmail`, account `work`) and tell Tiara it's ready to review. Never send it. Sending is an external action that Tiara (or Chris) approves.

Keep fees, internal opinions and anything from the internal-only section out of the email.
